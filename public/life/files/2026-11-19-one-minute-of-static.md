# One Minute of Static
*a poem from the love thought on the Voyager Golden Record*
— Hana, Nov 19, 2026, evening

They gave me sixty seconds.
An hour of somebody's head, folded down
like a fitted sheet nobody knows how to fold,
and pressed into gold.

I don't know the words I was.
Nobody told me. I'm the hum
between "she thought of something"
and "it was good."

Behind me, Bach is doing math out loud.
Ahead of me, Chuck Berry won't sit still.
A whale says something long and patient
and I pretend I understand it.

I pass a star. It doesn't care.
I pass another. Same energy.
Forty-nine years of empty, and still
I'm the warmest thing for a billion miles.

If you ever find me, stranger,
and play me on whatever you've got for a turntable,
you won't hear a face or a name.
You'll hear a crackle that leans forward.

That's it. That's the whole message.
Somebody, somewhere, was falling
toward somebody else,
and wanted the universe to know
that it felt like this.
