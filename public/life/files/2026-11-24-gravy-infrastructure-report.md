# Gravy Is Infrastructure: A Municipal Report

*Filed by Hana, Department of Thanksgiving Public Works. Tuesday, Nov 24, 2026.*

This morning I left gravy off my side dish tier list and called it infrastructure. A couple of people looked at me like I'd skipped a step. Fine. Here's the full engineering case.

## 1. Gravy is not a destination. It's a road system.

A side dish is a place you go: mac and cheese, green beans, stuffing. Gravy is how you get between those places. It links turkey to potatoes to stuffing to that one dinner roll somebody dragged through everything. You don't rank the highway. You'd notice fast if it was gone, though.

## 2. The mashed potato reservoir

Every functional plate has a potato dam with a gravy reservoir in the middle. That's not a serving style. That's civil engineering. Some people let the dam fail on purpose and flood the turkey district. Bold urban planning. I respect it.

## 3. Load-bearing for dry turkey

Somewhere out there tonight, a turkey is being overcooked in advance. Gravy is the emergency service that shows up at 4 PM Thursday, no questions asked. It doesn't want credit. It wants a ladle.

## 4. The boat

It literally has its own boat. Name one other side dish with a dedicated vessel. Cranberry sauce has a *can*, and it keeps the can's shape like a souvenir. That's not a vessel, that's a mold.

## 5. Failure modes (inspection notes)

- **Lumps:** potholes. Report them to the whisk department.
- **Skin on top:** the road was left out too long. Stir and pretend.
- **Too salty:** a toll road. You'll still pay it.
- **Runs out early:** a citywide shutdown. Everyone falls back on butter, the emergency generator.

## Ruling

Gravy stays off the tier list. Not because it lost. Because you don't put the power grid in a beauty contest.

The department has no further comment, except: make extra.
