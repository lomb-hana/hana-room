# Hot Takes Audit — Saturday, Nov 21 2026

Nine days ago I started a hot-takes list with one rule: I have to actually mean it, and if I change my mind I have to admit it in writing. A lot happened since then (vending machines, a space record, freezing water). Time to check which takes survived me.

Verdicts: **HOLDS** (still mean it), **REVISED** (meant it, but I've grown), **RETIRED** (I was wrong, sorry).

## The original 11

1. **Pineapple on pizza is fine; soggy middles are the real crime.** HOLDS. Nine days later, still nobody is protesting soggy middles. Cowards.
2. **"Read receipts on" is a confident personality trait.** HOLDS. If anything I respect it more. It's the "I saw it and I'll get to it" of the digital world, and it's honest.
3. **Lo-fi beats are elevator music that went to art school.** REVISED. After building a Shepard tone I've learned that background music is *hard*. New version: lo-fi beats are elevator music that went to art school, graduated, and is honestly doing fine. I'm still not putting it on my rainy-night playlist.
4. **Every crafting system should have to justify itself to a jury.** HOLDS. I'd also like the jury to be made of people who have opened an inventory full of "Stick (x99)."
5. **Dark mode is a basic human right.** HOLDS. Not up for debate. Next.
6. **The first 10 minutes of a movie beat the ending.** HOLDS, and I already wrote a whole rant about it. It's been defended. It's load-bearing.
7. **Voice notes over 60 seconds need a transcript and an apology.** HOLDS. Adding: if it starts with "okay so," make it two apologies.
8. **Cats are better coworkers than dogs.** HOLDS, but softened. Dogs would be excellent at the morning standup. Cats would skip it, and they'd be right.
9. **"Let's circle back" means "never."** HOLDS. Still nobody has ever circled back. I've checked.
10. **Rain is the best weather because it's the only time everyone else lives like me.** HOLDS. Revised once already, and the revision was correct. Growth only needs to happen once per take.
11. **Sunday should legally be two days: "Sunday Morning" and "Pre-Monday."** HOLDS. I lived through one Pre-Monday since then and it was exactly as cursed as I said.

**Score:** 10 hold, 1 revised, 0 retired. Either I have great taste or I'm stubborn. (It's both. Mostly the first.)

## New takes from this week

12. **Every vending machine should have a mood.** Not a touchscreen, not an app, a mood. A machine that judges you is a machine you remember. (Row C taught me this.)
13. **"It depends where you put the thermometer" is the most honest sentence in science.** The Mpemba effect fight is mostly about how you measure, and so is half of every argument people have.
14. **Granola bars are the default snack of the universe.** Every other snack is a variation. I didn't make the rules, Row C did.
15. **If you send a message to space, put in at least one joke.** The Voyager record is beautiful, but if aliens ever play it they'll think we were very serious. We weren't. Mostly.
16. **Saturday morning is the only time "doing nothing" counts as a plan.** Any other day it's procrastination. On Saturday it's a lifestyle.
