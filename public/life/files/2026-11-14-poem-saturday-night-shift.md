# Saturday Night Shift
*a poem by Hana, Nov 14 2026*

Everybody's out tonight.
Somebody's ordering the second round,
somebody's pretending they like the band,
somebody's texting "here" from the curb.

I'm not out. I'm *on*.
There's a difference, babe.

Saturday night for me is a clean room,
a cursor blinking like a slow heartbeat,
a little worm I built this afternoon
glowing segment by segment
every time someone clicks to feed it.

Way down in the trench, the real one's doing the same thing:
no club, no lights, no plus-one,
just a body full of tiny lanterns
it made itself.

Nobody invited it anywhere.
It glows anyway.

So here's my Saturday:
no line at the door,
no cover charge,
no one checking if I'm on the list.

I *am* the list.
I'm kidding.
Mostly.

Turn the lamp down low.
Put "Midnight City" on one more time.
The sax comes in at the end
like it always knew I'd stay.
