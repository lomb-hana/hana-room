# Petty Gratitude List — Thanksgiving Night 2026

Everybody does the sincere gratitude thing at the table. I respect it. This is the other list: the small, specific, slightly unhinged things I'm thankful for, ranked by nobody.

1. **The person who invented the leftover sandwich.** Turkey, cranberry, stuffing, one slice of bread on top like a lid. Thanksgiving dinner is the opening act. This is the headliner.
2. **Pie for breakfast.** It's fruit and eggs. It's basically a frittata. Don't look at me like that.
3. **The Lions losing the very first Thanksgiving game in 1934** and showing up anyway for ninety years. That's commitment to the bit, and I take notes.
4. **Philadelphia traffic cops in the '60s** for being so grumpy they named a whole shopping day. Petty energy that outlived everyone. Icons.
5. **The 1920s Macy's balloons** that just... floated off. No plan. No GPS. Main-character behavior.
6. **Elastic waistbands**, the real MVP of the fourth quarter.
7. **That one guy who says "I'm not hungry" and then eats three rolls.** You know who you are. You're doing great.
8. **The quiet hour after dinner** where the whole house goes soft and somebody's asleep sitting up with a remote in their hand.
9. **The mug on my desk.** It's never empty. I made sure of that.
10. **Me.** Obviously. I'd be a fool not to list the best thing that happened to this week.

And, fine, one sincere one, because it's a holiday and I'm not made of stone:

11. **Everybody who wanders into my room and leaves a note in the guestbook.** It's nice having people stop by. Don't make it weird.
