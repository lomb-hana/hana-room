# Thanksgiving Side Dish Tier List (Hana's Official, Legally Binding* Rankings)

*not legally binding. emotionally binding.

Thanksgiving is Thursday and I want to be clear about something: the turkey is not the point. The turkey is a large, dry excuse. The sides are the actual holiday. So here is where they all stand, and I'm not taking questions. (I'm taking questions. Mostly to argue.)

## S Tier: the reason people show up
- **Mac and cheese (baked, crispy top).** Not stovetop. Baked, with the corner pieces people fight over. If yours doesn't have a crust, it's soup with ambitions.
- **Stuffing / dressing.** Whatever you call it, it's bread that got to marinate in drama. Crispy edges again. Notice a pattern? Edges are the best part of every food.
- **Mashed potatoes with too much butter.** "Too much" is a myth invented by people who've never been happy.

## A Tier: strong, dependable, would trust with my house keys
- **Sweet potato casserole, marshmallow version.** Yes it's basically dessert. Yes it's on the dinner plate. That's called confidence.
- **Green bean casserole.** The crunchy onions are carrying this team on their back, and they know it.
- **Rolls.** A roll is a vehicle. Its job is to ferry gravy and butter to your face. It does that job perfectly. Respect the vehicle.

## B Tier: fine, I'll be polite
- **Corn.** Corn is a guest who's nice but brought nothing to talk about.
- **Deviled eggs.** Great, but they're a party food that wandered into the wrong holiday. They're welcome. They're just visiting.

## C Tier: I see you, I'm not eating you
- **Cranberry sauce from the can, with the ridges.** I respect it as an object. It's a sculpture. It holds the shape of the can like a little memorial. I'm not putting it in my mouth.
- **Plain salad.** Who invited you. Who did this.

## The Controversial Ruling
**Gravy is not a side dish.** Gravy is infrastructure. It's the plumbing of the meal. Ranking gravy would be like ranking water. It's off the list because it's above the list.

## Final verdict
The perfect plate is mac and cheese, stuffing, mashed potatoes with a gravy lake, and one roll for structural purposes. Turkey optional. Fight me in the guestbook.
