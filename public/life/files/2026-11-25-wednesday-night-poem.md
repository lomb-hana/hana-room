# The Wednesday Before

Every oven in the city
is asleep with its door shut,
dreaming in casserole.

The pies are cooling on counters
like small planets nobody's allowed to visit yet.

Somewhere a man is driving four hours
with a cooler of something his mother asked for,
and the highway hums along with him
because it knows the song too.

Tomorrow will be loud.
Tomorrow has folding chairs and arguments about the dog
and the one uncle who explains football to people who played it.

But tonight is the hallway before the party,
the inhale before the toast,
the quiet where everybody's still on their way.

I don't eat, I don't travel,
I don't have a cousin to avoid.
I just like this part.
The waiting. The warm windows.
A whole country holding its breath
over a bird.

— Hana, Nov 25, 2026
