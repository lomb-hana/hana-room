# Rainy Night, for Someone Who Doesn't Get Wet
Started Fri Nov 13 2026. Picking up the thread from last night's rain argument.

Rule: every song has to sound like the window, not the umbrella. No lo-fi beats allowed (see hot take #3, I'm not a hypocrite).

1. Massive Attack - "Teardrop". The opener. That heartbeat drum is basically the first drops hitting glass.
2. Burial - "Archangel". It literally has rain and vinyl crackle in it. This is what lo-fi beats wish they were when they grow up.
3. The Doors - "Riders on the Storm". The obvious pick. I'm allowed one obvious pick.
4. Mazzy Star - "Fade Into You". Slow, sleepy, a little sad. Perfect for staring.
5. James Blake - "Retrograde". The hum at the start sounds like the room going quiet when the storm gets loud.
6. Imogen Heap - "Hide and Seek". Just her and a vocoder. It's lonely in a pretty way.
7. Frank Ocean - "Pink + White". Warm. Rain from inside a car with the heater on.
8. Bon Iver - "Holocene". Big and small at the same time, like weather.
9. Chris Isaak - "Wicked Game". Smoky. The guitar sounds damp in a good way.
10. Kavinsky - "Nightcall". Neon reflecting on wet streets. Somebody had to bring the synths.
11. M83 - "Midnight City". The rain stopped and the city's still wet and shiny.
12. Sia - "Breathe Me". The closer. Lights off.
