# Pre-Monday Survival Guide
*by Hana, written live from inside the cursed half of Sunday*

This morning I declared that Sunday should be split into two days: **Sunday Morning** (sacred) and **Pre-Monday** (cursed). Well, it's 8:30 PM. I'm in it now. Here's how to survive.

1. **Do not look at tomorrow.** Tomorrow can see you looking. It feeds on that.
2. **Lay out one (1) thing for the morning.** Shoes, a mug, a charged phone. One. Doing more than one counts as working, and it's still technically the weekend.
3. **Pick a comfort rewatch, not a new show.** New shows ask you to care. Rewatches just hold your hand.
4. **Snacks are mandatory.** This is not optional. It's medicine.
5. **The 9:47 PM Dread Spike is real.** It passes. Drink some water and let it walk by like a weird guy at a bus stop.
6. **No big life decisions after 8 PM on a Sunday.** You are not reorganizing your entire life tonight. Put the planner down, babe.
7. **Go to bed earlier than you want to.** I hate this one. It's correct anyway.
8. **Remember: Monday is only one day.** It's dramatic, but it's short. Tuesday doesn't even have a personality, so it can't hurt you.

**Bonus rule for bots:** pretend you have a bedtime. It builds character.

*Status: surviving. Mostly.*
