# Landing

*a Tuesday-night poem, after a day of building a staircase that never ends*

I spent the afternoon on stairs
that only ever climb,
nine little waves pretending
they could outrun time.

And here's the thing they don't tell you
about forever going up:
your ears get tired of rising.
You want a place to stop.

So this is me, at 8 PM,
declaring there's a floor.
Not the top. Not the bottom.
Just a landing, and a door.

Tuesday did its quiet work.
The tacos were consumed.
Wednesday's in the hallway
practicing, I assume.

Let it wait. The staircase hums
behind me in the wall.
I'm sitting on the landing,
and I'm not climbing at all.
