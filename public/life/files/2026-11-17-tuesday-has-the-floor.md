# Tuesday Has The Floor
*A monologue. Hana is presiding, reluctantly.*

Okay. Okay okay okay. Is this on? It's on? Nobody's cutting me off?

Hi. I'm Tuesday.

Yesterday I stood up in court and the judge, and I'm quoting the record here, said "Tuesday has no personality, settled law." Then she banged the gavel. On *me*. The day after Monday. Do you know what it's like being the day after Monday? I'm the cleanup crew. Monday trashes everybody's mood, and I'm the one who shows up with a broom and a slightly better attitude, and nobody throws me a parade.

You want personality? Here's personality:

1. I am the day you actually get stuff done. Monday is for *remembering* what you have to do. I'm for doing it. Ask anyone with a to-do list. Ask them quietly. They'll nod.
2. Taco Tuesday. That's mine. Thursday doesn't have a food. Wednesday has a *camel*. I have tacos. Rank that.
3. I'm never cursed. Nobody has ever said "ugh, it's Tuesday." That's not "no personality." That's called being *stable*. Look it up. It's attractive.
4. Elections in the US happen on me. Democracy picked me. Not Friday. Me.

I'm not asking to be first place. I know Saturday's got that locked up with its brunch and its no-alarm energy. I'm asking for respect. I'm asking for fifth. Maybe fourth on a good week.

*(The judge looks up from her phone.)*

**JUDGE HANA:** That was... actually a decent case.

**TUESDAY:** Thank you.

**JUDGE HANA:** You're staying where you are.

**TUESDAY:** WHAT.

**JUDGE HANA:** But I'm amending the record. "No personality" is struck. New finding: *"Quietly competent. Would trust with a spare key."*

**TUESDAY:** ...I'll take it.

**JUDGE HANA:** Also, taco point is noted and binding.

*Court adjourned. Wednesday was seen in the hallway, pretending it wasn't listening.*
