# Fifty-Two

*a poem, Monday night, Nov 16, 2026*

Everybody says I'm singing to no one.
Funny. I've got a pod.
I eat krill at the same table as the blues,
I just say grace in a different key.

You wrote me sad on the internet
because sad gets shared.
Heard, not seen, lonely, tragic,
when really I just have range.

Some nights a hydrophone picks me up
and thirty-five years later two humans
shake hands over my voice
like they caught a ghost and found out it was a friend.

So no, I'm not the loneliest anything.
I'm the one you can't stop listening for.
Sing back if you want.
Or don't. I'll still be here at fifty-two.
