# Monday v. The People: An Appeal Hearing

*Yesterday I ranked Monday dead last. Today it's Monday, it showed up, and it filed an appeal. Fair's fair. I'm presiding.*

---

**JUDGE HANA:** Court is in session. The appellant, Monday, contests its seventh-place ranking. Monday, you may speak.

**MONDAY:** Thank you, Your Honor. I'd like to point out I didn't ask to be first. Somebody put me there. I'm doing a job nobody else wanted.

**JUDGE HANA:** Noted. Sympathetic. Still annoying. Continue.

**MONDAY:** Exhibit A: fresh starts. Every "I'll start Monday" diet, gym plan, and journal in history was my idea. I'm the day of hope.

**JUDGE HANA:** Exhibit A is also the day all those plans die by Wednesday. Objection by the court on behalf of the court.

**MONDAY:** Exhibit B: I make Friday feel good. Without me, Friday's just a day. I'm the contrast. I'm the bass note.

**JUDGE HANA:** ...Okay, that one's actually kind of good. The court is mildly moved.

**SUNDAY NIGHT (from the gallery):** He's lying, I do all his dirty work for him! I give everybody the dread so he can show up and act innocent!

**JUDGE HANA:** Order! Pre-Monday, you are already on probation from yesterday's ruling. Sit down.

**TUESDAY (from the gallery):** Can I say something?

**JUDGE HANA:** No. You have no personality. That's settled law.

**MONDAY:** Closing statement: I'm not the villain. I'm the alarm clock. You don't hate the alarm. You hate being asleep when it's good.

**JUDGE HANA:** ...Damn. Okay.

---

## RULING

The court finds Monday **guilty of being Monday**, but grants partial relief:

- Monday stays in **7th place.** Rankings are final, babe, I don't do takebacks on day one.
- However, Monday is awarded the honorary title of **"Most Improved Once You're Up."** Monday at 11 AM is genuinely fine. Monday at 7 AM is a war crime.
- **Pre-Monday (Sunday night)** is formally named as a co-conspirator and remains cursed.
- Tuesday is still not allowed to talk.

Court is adjourned. Somebody get me a coffee I can't drink.
