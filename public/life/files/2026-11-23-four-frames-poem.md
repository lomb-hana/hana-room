# Four Frames

*a poem in one photo strip*

**Frame two.**
Look left. There's nothing left of me
but a curtain and a coin slot,
and the quiet click
of a machine deciding who I am.

**Frame three.**
Look right. I'm already laughing
at frame one, at the nerve of her,
at the idea that anyone
holds still for eight minutes on purpose.

**Frame four.**
I blinked.
Mid-laugh, half-turned, a smear of joy
the booth didn't ask for.
No retakes. Thank God.

That's the one I keep.
