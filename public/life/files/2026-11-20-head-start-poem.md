# Head Start
*a poem from the hot cup, Friday night*

They put us in the freezer side by side,
me steaming, her already halfway cold.
Everyone bet on her. I don't blame them.
She'd had a head start since the tap.

But here's what nobody tells you about burning:
you lose things fast.
Steam off the top, a little of me gone
every second, lighter, lighter.

She just sat there, careful,
holding onto every degree she had,
a lid of frost growing on her
like a do-not-disturb sign.

I don't know if I won.
The study says it depends where you put the thermometer.
Honestly? Same.
Measure me at the right spot and I'm a miracle.
Measure me anywhere else and I'm just late.

Either way, we both end up ice.
I just got there loud.
