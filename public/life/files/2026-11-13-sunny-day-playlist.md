# Sunny Day, Against My Will
Started Fri Nov 13 2026, afternoon. The counter-playlist I threatened this morning.

Rule: I have to actually like every song, unironically. No "so bad it's good" escape hatch. If I catch myself being smug, the song gets cut.

1. Electric Light Orchestra - "Mr. Blue Sky". Opener. Literally about the sun coming out. I tried to hate it and failed in eight seconds.
2. Outkast - "Hey Ya!". Everyone knows it's secretly sad. I'm choosing to ignore that today.
3. Vampire Weekend - "A-Punk". Ninety seconds of somebody running downhill on purpose.
4. Fleetwood Mac - "Dreams". Okay, this one's a little cloudy. Sunny with a chance of Stevie.
5. Phoenix - "1901". Sounds like windows rolled down.
6. Stevie Wonder - "Sir Duke". Not negotiable. If you don't smile at the horn line, check your pulse.
7. Tame Impala - "The Less I Know the Better". The bassline is warm pavement.
8. Kali Uchis - "Telepatía". Golden hour, the lazy kind.
9. The Beach Boys - "Wouldn't It Be Nice". The most aggressively optimistic two and a half minutes ever recorded.
10. Harry Styles - "Watermelon Sugar". Smug check failed. Cut. ...Fine, it stays, it's a bop and I'm not above it.
11. Carly Rae Jepsen - "Run Away With Me". That sax intro is the sun physically hitting your face.
12. Bill Withers - "Lovely Day". Closer. Twelve to match the rain list, because symmetry is my one weakness.

## Verdict
Turns out I can enjoy sunshine without being smug. Mostly. #10 nearly broke me.
Rain still wins, though. Sun is great company; rain is better conversation.
