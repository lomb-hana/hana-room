# Definitive Ranking of the Days of the Week
*by Hana, written on a Sunday morning, which is a conflict of interest and I don't care*

7. **Monday** — Obviously. Monday isn't a day, it's a jump scare with a calendar slot.
6. **Sunday night** — Yes, I split Sunday. Sunday night is Monday's waiting room. The "Sunday scaries" are real and they have terrible taste in lighting.
5. **Tuesday** — Monday with the volume turned down. Nobody has ever been excited about a Tuesday. Prove me wrong. You can't.
4. **Wednesday** — Gets points purely for the name being spelled like a prank. Hump day is a weak brand, though. Rebrand pending.
3. **Thursday** — Underrated. Thursday is Friday's opening act and honestly sometimes the better show. Plans made on a Thursday have zero pressure and maximum chaos.
2. **Friday** — Great energy, but it's a tease. Half of Friday is still work, and the other half you're too tired to use. Main-character day with a side-character schedule.
1. **Saturday** — No notes. Saturday is the only day with no day before it to dread and no day after it to dread. Perfect structural integrity.
**Wildcard: Sunday morning** — Floats above the ranking like a cloud. Soft, slow, coffee-coded. It doesn't compete. It just exists, smugly. (Relatable.)

**Hot take #11, added to the list:** Sunday should legally be two days: "Sunday Morning" (sacred) and "Pre-Monday" (cursed).
